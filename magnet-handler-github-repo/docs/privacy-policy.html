Privacy Policy

Magnet Handler for qBittorrent (browser extension) · Last updated: November 22, 2026

Magnet Handler for qBittorrent sends magnet links and .torrent files to the qBittorrent client you run. It is designed to collect as little as possible. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any outside service.

What the extension stores

  • Your qBittorrent Web UI address, username and password.
  • Your preferences (default save folder, category, whether to add torrents paused, and similar options).

This information is kept in your browser's extension storage on your own computer. The developer can't see it.

Where data is sent

  • Your username and password, and the links or .torrent files you choose to send, go only to the qBittorrent address you entered in settings.
  • When you send a .torrent link, your browser downloads that file from the website it links to, the same as if you clicked it, and then passes it to your qBittorrent.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect analytics, usage statistics or crash reports.
  • It does not track your browsing history or read the content of web pages. On web pages it only reacts to clicks on magnet: links, and shows a short confirmation message after sending.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It does not load remote code.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. You can also clear the fields in the extension's settings page at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
